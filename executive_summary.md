# Executive Summary: HGMN Risk Register

*Fictional company, simulated evidence. 12 November 2026.*

## Overall picture

HGMN faces 8 High and 2 Medium risks today across its broadcast, digital and journalist-facing operations. If the planned controls are delivered, 8 of the 10 fall to Medium and 2 remain High (R03, R06).

## Top five risks today

1. **R05 (High, 16):** An official social media account is taken over through phishing and used to post false statements.
2. **R06 (High, 16):** A journalist's phone or laptop is compromised by targeted spear-phishing or spyware.
3. **R10 (High, 16):** An intrusion goes undetected for days because there is no 24/7 monitoring and the incident response plan has never been tested.
4. **R02 (High, 15):** Ransomware on the broadcast network takes playout off-air for more than 4 hours.
5. **R03 (High, 15):** A confidential journalist source identity is exposed through a compromised tool or an insider.

## Three actions to start this month

1. **Control who can publish.** Review all freelancer and contractor accounts, and require stronger sign-in for the CMS and official social media accounts (R01, R05).
2. **Protect the broadcast chain.** Document a patch process for broadcast systems and test a playout restore (R02).
3. **Protect sources.** Define how source-protection data is classified and who may access it (R03).

## Decisions needed from leadership

- Approve funding and ownership for broadcast network segmentation and monitoring.
- Formally accept or reject the bureau physical-security risk (R09). The COO is the proposed approver.
- Confirm the risk appetite for source-exposure risk, which stays High after planned controls because the impact is Severe.
