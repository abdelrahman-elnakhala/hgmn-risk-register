# Methodology

All content relates to the fictional company Horizon Gulf Media Network (HGMN).

## Likelihood

| Level | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | Less than once in 5 years |
| 2 | Unlikely | Once in 2 to 5 years |
| 3 | Possible | About once a year |
| 4 | Likely | Several times a year |
| 5 | Almost certain | Monthly or more |

## Impact (use the highest dimension that applies)

Dimensions: broadcast continuity, source and journalist safety, editorial integrity, legal and regulatory, financial.

| Level | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minor | No broadcast effect; internal only |
| 2 | Limited | Digital outage under 1 hour |
| 3 | Moderate | A channel degraded 1 to 4 hours, or limited data exposure |
| 4 | Major | Off-air over 4 hours, false content published, or notable PII breach |
| 5 | Severe | Source identity exposed, prolonged blackout, or licence or regulatory action |

## Rating bands

| Rating | Score range |
|---|---|
| Low | 1 to 4 |
| Medium | 5 to 9 |
| High | 10 to 16 |
| Critical | 17 to 25 |

## Rules

1. Score likelihood and impact separately, with a one-line reason for each.
2. Inherent risk assumes only the controls that exist today.
3. Residual risk assumes the planned controls are implemented. An *Accept* decision keeps residual equal to inherent.
4. Treatment options: mitigate, transfer, accept, avoid. Every accepted risk needs a named approver and a review date.
5. Review each risk at least every six months and after any related incident.

## Limits

Scales and scores are judgment-based and simulated. A real programme would calibrate them with the business and test them against incident history.
